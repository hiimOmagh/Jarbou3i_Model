## Scope

The whole workbench (index.html and src/ui/*.css): setup flow, model map, case file, review, dialogs. Mode: Operate. Full restyle; product truth, copy, behaviour and controls stay.

## Audience and job

Curious everyday readers first, then researchers, journalists, analysts and students (PRODUCT.md). Job: set up an analysis, paste the AI's answer, then read and check it layer by layer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An analysis is a line you can ride: each layer a station, each link an interchange, your place always ringed. It refuses the AI-tool default of navy panels, cyan glow, mono uppercase eyebrows and boxed cards.

OWN-WORLD: Platform white ground (#F8F8F6), enamel-white panels, ink #15171B. Six line colours (red #D62B2B, blue #1F5BC4, yellow #E9A21A, green #1D8A4E, purple #7A3F9E, ink), plus orange, teal and brown for the nine Biopolitical layers. Stations are white rings with a 4px ink edge; the current one gets a second, coloured ring. Ink pill buttons, 2px ink field edges, thick ink rules in place of card boxes. One type family; hierarchy by size and weight only. The dark theme is the midnight-enamel version of the same map, never black with neon.

STORY: A newcomer sees where they are and what comes next. They trust the map because segment lengths are the real record counts. They ride the analysis station by station.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A white header bar with a 3px ink rule, holding the mascot, title and lead. The setup's five steps form a vertical line of stations, the current one ringed, with the guide's speech above them. The step heading is large and the topic field has a 2px ink edge. The primary action is an ink pill at the end of the step.

FORM: Transit diagram (Beck/Vignelli lineage), position 1 on the list (the roll). seed:4a2c83ac4397e33b. Signature: the model map drawn as one continuous line, its segment lengths set by each layer's record count. On a phone, one station per screen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
