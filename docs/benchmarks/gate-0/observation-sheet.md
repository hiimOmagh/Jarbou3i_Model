## Separation rule

### Participant explanations — verbatim

Record what the participant said caused difficulty or informed a decision.

### Observer inferences

Record interpretations only after the task. Tie each inference to event IDs or
timestamps and state a competing explanation.

### Proposed responses

Do not write a design solution until observations and competing explanations
are recorded. Every proposal must state what later evidence would disprove it.
